Privacy

Visiting the site

I count page views with my own server-side counter. Your IP address is hashed together with your browser string and the date, so it can't be traced back to you or linked across days. There are no cookies for visitors and no third-party trackers.

Using the contact form

I store your name, email address, message and IP address. I use them to reply and to limit spam. New messages are also posted to a private Discord channel so I get a notification. I keep messages until I delete them, and you can ask me to remove yours by emailing me.
